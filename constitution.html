CONSTITUTION

REPUBLIC OF OMEGA

“Together We Grow.”

PREAMBLE

We, the members of the Republic of Omega, united by a shared vision of community, learning, creativity, innovation, peace and progress, establish this Constitution as the foundational framework for the organization and governance of Omega.

We commit ourselves to responsible citizenship, equality, cooperation, transparency and respect for one another.

ARTICLE I — THE REPUBLIC

1. The name of this community shall be the Republic of Omega.

2. Omega is a voluntary micronation and civic community project.

3. Omega does not claim sovereignty over territory belonging to any existing sovereign state.

4. Participation in Omega does not replace or cancel the citizenship or legal obligations of any member's country of residence or nationality.

ARTICLE II — CITIZENSHIP

1. Membership in Omega shall be voluntary.

2. Any person who accepts the principles and rules of Omega may apply for membership, subject to the procedures established by the government of Omega.

3. Members shall respect the rights and dignity of other members.

ARTICLE III — FUNDAMENTAL RIGHTS

Members of Omega shall have the right to:

  • Equal and respectful treatment.
  • Express opinions peacefully.
  • Participate in community activities.
  • Propose ideas and improvements.
  • Participate in eligible elections and civic processes.
  • Access information concerning the governance of Omega.

ARTICLE IV — GOVERNMENT

1. The government of Omega shall operate according to this Constitution.

2. Government institutions shall promote transparency, responsibility and service to the community.

3. The structure and powers of government shall be defined by constitutional provisions and laws adopted under them.

ARTICLE V — LEADERSHIP

1. Leaders of Omega shall serve the interests of the community.

2. Leadership positions shall be subject to rules established by the Constitution and applicable laws of Omega.

3. No leader shall have unlimited authority.

ARTICLE VI — ELECTIONS

1. Eligible members may participate in elections established by Omega.

2. Elections shall be conducted according to transparent rules.

3. Every eligible member shall have equal voting rights in elections applicable to their membership level.

ARTICLE VII — NATIONAL SYMBOLS

The official symbols of Omega may include:

  • The Flag of the Republic of Omega.
  • The National Emblem.
  • The National Motto.
  • The National Anthem.

These symbols shall represent the identity, values and unity of the Omega community.

ARTICLE VIII — AMENDMENTS

This Constitution may be amended through a transparent constitutional process approved by the appropriate governing authority and, where required, the members of Omega.

ARTICLE IX — RESPONSIBILITY

All members and institutions of Omega shall respect the laws of the countries and jurisdictions in which they operate.

ARTICLE X — FINAL PROVISION

This Constitution shall serve as the foundational framework for the Republic of Omega and its community.
