GOVERNMENT OF OMEGA

Republic of Omega — Together We Grow.

Our Government

The government of the Republic of Omega exists to organize the community, protect its principles, encourage participation, and promote the welfare and development of its members.

Government institutions operate according to the Constitution of Omega and the rules adopted under it.

Omega is a voluntary micronation and civic community project. Its institutions do not replace the lawful governments or legal obligations of the countries in which its members live.

01

President of Omega

The President shall serve as the Head of State of the Republic of Omega.

Responsibilities

  • Represent the Republic of Omega.
  • Uphold the Constitution.
  • Promote unity among members.
  • Appoint or recognize officials according to Omega's laws.
  • Perform official ceremonial duties.
02

Prime Minister

The Prime Minister shall serve as the Head of Government and oversee the administration of Omega.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate government departments.
  • Implement approved policies.
  • Coordinate government programs.
  • Work with the National Assembly.
  • Report on government activities.
03

National Assembly

The National Assembly shall be the representative legislative institution of Omega.

Responsibilities

  • Debate proposed laws.
  • Approve community rules and policies.
  • Represent the interests of members.
  • Review government activities.
  • Participate in constitutional processes.
04

Constitutional Council

The Constitutional Council shall help protect the principles and framework established by the Constitution.

Responsibilities

  • Review constitutional questions.
  • Advise government institutions.
  • Promote constitutional compliance.
  • Help resolve constitutional disputes.
05

Citizens and Members

Members are the foundation of the Republic of Omega. They may participate in eligible elections, community discussions, projects and other civic activities.

Member Responsibilities

  • Respect the Constitution.
  • Respect other members.
  • Participate responsibly.
  • Follow applicable Omega rules.
  • Respect the laws of their actual country of residence.
06

Government Departments

As Omega develops, specialized departments may be established to manage specific areas of community activity.

  • Department of Education
  • Department of Technology
  • Department of Culture
  • Department of Finance
  • Department of Communications
  • Department of Community Development
