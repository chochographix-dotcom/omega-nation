FOUNDING DECLARATION

Republic of Omega

Motto: Together We Grow.

We, the people and members of the Republic of Omega, establish this community as a voluntary micronation and civic project founded upon unity, responsibility, creativity, learning, innovation, peace and progress.

Omega exists to provide a community where individuals can participate in a shared national identity, contribute ideas, develop their talents and work together toward a better future.

Our Principles

  • Unity and respect for all members.
  • Freedom of thought, expression and creativity.
  • Equality and fair treatment.
  • Education, innovation and personal development.
  • Peaceful cooperation with other communities.
  • Responsible leadership and transparent governance.
  • Respect for the laws of the countries in which our members live.

The Purpose of Omega

The Republic of Omega seeks to build a strong, organized and positive community through participation, service, creativity, education and responsible citizenship.

Omega does not claim sovereignty over territory belonging to another state. Membership is voluntary and participation in Omega does not replace citizenship of any existing country.

Our Commitment

We commit ourselves to building Omega through cooperation, honesty, responsibility and continuous improvement.

Together We Grow.
